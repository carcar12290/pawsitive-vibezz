  Our Stay at Walt Disney World During a Hurricane
Before Hurricane Ian

Our experience during Hurricane Ian while staying on Disney World property.

Imagine this: you're nestled in the lap of luxury at Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa, excitedly planning your magical days ahead at the happiest place on earth. But suddenly, news of Hurricane Ian hits, throwing your vacation plans into uncertainty. What do you do?

Staying at Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa during Hurricane Ian was an unexpected adventure, to say the least. Known for its Victorian elegance and impeccable service, the resort itself became a safe haven amidst the storm. Here’s a glimpse into our experience and some tips if you ever find yourself in a similar situation:

Comfort Amidst Chaos

The first thing that struck us was the calm and reassuring atmosphere maintained by the resort staff. Despite the storm brewing outside, the interiors of the Grand Floridian remained serene and well-prepared. The hotel’s sturdy construction and hurricane protocols ensured that guests felt safe and secure throughout. 

Adaptability and Support

Disney's dedication to guest experience truly shone during Hurricane Ian. They kept us updated with timely information about the storm’s progression and any necessary precautions. The resort staff went above and beyond, ensuring everyone had essential supplies, from making sure we could access Sandy Cove Gifts and Sundries for snacks and merchandise, Gasparilla Island Grill for quick-service food, special dining options at Citricos, and even organizing activities indoors to keep spirits high. We were even able to purchase playing cards at M. Mouse Mercantile. There was an assigned cast member for our building if we needed anything. They helped us wash any laundry we needed cleaned, and brought over a microwave from a DVC room to use for our leftovers. Now keep in mind that microwaves are only available in DVC rooms. They will try to provide one if you ask and if any are available. There was always a cast member around if assistance was needed.

Making the Most of It                                                             Thursday when it was safe to go back outside

While some planned outdoor activities were canceled due to the weather, Disney's Grand Floridian offered plenty to keep us entertained indoors. From recreational activities in the lobby set up by the recreational cast members, including games and a DJ, to character experiences that delighted both kids and adults alike, there was no shortage of magical moments to be had. After the storm had passed and everything was cleaned up, we were able to head to the parks one last time before our departure. What is amazing is that Disney does so well with preparing; you wouldn’t have known after the fact that there was a hurricane. At least in our experience!

Tips for Future Visitors

If you find yourself at Disney's Grand Floridian during hurricane season, here are a few tips:

  • Stay Informed: Keep updated with the latest weather forecasts and instructions from the resort staff.
  • Stay Stocked: Have essentials like water, snacks, and any necessary medications on hand.
  • Stay Flexible: Understand that some activities may be altered or canceled due to weather conditions, but there's always something magical to do indoors at Disney.

Conclusion

Despite the unexpected turn of events, our stay at Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa during Hurricane Ian turned out to be an unforgettable experience. The combination of Disney magic and exemplary hospitality made a potentially stressful situation surprisingly enjoyable. Whether you're visiting during hurricane season or not, one thing remains certain: Disney's Grand Floridian is a haven where luxury meets resilience, ensuring your stay is nothing short of enchanting.

Have you experienced a stay at Disney's Grand Floridian or another Disney resort during a storm? Share your stories and tips in the comments below!
