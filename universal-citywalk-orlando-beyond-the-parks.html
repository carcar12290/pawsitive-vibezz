Universal CityWalk Orlando...Beyond The Parks

2026-03-04

 



Have you ever been to Universal Orlando Resort and loved the parks… but wished you could soak up a little more Universal fun without using a park ticket? Or maybe you’re heading to Orlando and the parks just aren’t in the budget this time. Good news — you can still get a taste of the action!

Welcome to CityWalk — Universal’s lively hub of dining, shopping, and entertainment.

The best part? CityWalk itself is FREE to explore. If you’re driving in (and not staying at a Universal resort), you’ll just pay for parking. Going after 6 pm, parking is typically FREE on most nights. From there, the night is yours. Stroll the promenade, pop into shops, enjoy the music, and take in that electric theme park energy — no ticket required.

Didn’t make it into the parks but still want that epic souvenir? You can shop official merch at the massive Universal Studios Store and grab that tee, mug, or wand you had your eye on. Problem solved!

🍴 Come Hungry

CityWalk is basically a foodie playground.

One of the most popular spots is The Toothsome Chocolate Emporium & Savory Feast Kitchen. With its whimsical steampunk flair and over-the-top milkshakes, it’s a feast for both your eyes and your sweet tooth. It’s a family favorite — and for good reason!

If you’re craving something fun and funky, Voodoo Doughnut is our go-to sweet treat stop. These aren’t your average donuts — think wild toppings, bright colors, and flavors you didn’t even know you needed.

Looking for bold flavors? Bob Marley–A Tribute to Freedom serves up incredible Jamaican-inspired dishes in a vibrant, music-filled atmosphere. Prefer to keep it classic? You can grab familiar favorites at the Burger King Whopper Bar. Truly, there’s something for every palate.


🎯 Let the Games Begin

CityWalk isn’t just about food — it’s packed with entertainment, too.

Challenge the family to a round at Hollywood Drive-In Golf, featuring two interactive 18-hole mini golf courses themed around 1950s sci-fi and horror flicks. It’s colorful, quirky, and competitive in the best way. Bonus: you can take home a unique souvenir golf ball — even one that lights up! I still have mine… and it brings back the best memories. 😉

Feeling adventurous? Try your skills at Universal's Great Movie Escape, including the Back to the Future: OUTATIME experience. Work together, beat the clock, and see if your crew has what it takes to escape in time. Talk about a family bonding moment!


🎶 Soak It All In

Even if you’re just grabbing a coffee or a quick snack, CityWalk is worth a visit. Live entertainment, upbeat music, and the buzz of happy vacationers make it an experience all on its own.

Be sure to check the official CityWalk calendar before your trip to see what special events or performances are happening — it’s a great way to plan ahead and make the most of your visit.

No matter your style — foodie, shopper, adventurer, or just there for the vibes — CityWalk truly has something for everyone.

And remember… You don’t need a park ticket to make magical memories. ✨

As always, have a magical day!
