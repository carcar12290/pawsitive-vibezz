When life brings you highs and lows....

Life isn't always going to be perfect; let's face it, it just isn't. 

In our everyday lives, we have highs, middles, and lows; that is okay. The hard part about today's times is that we live in the age of social media. It is everywhere, from Instagram to YouTube. We use social media to connect with family and friends, and even see what celebrities are up to. Some of us use it for our jobs and/or side gigs. However it is used, it's there. We see all kinds of people, and we see their "happy, beautiful, fun, special lives," and we catch ourselves saying, "Oh, must be nice, or I wish I could do that." I am guilty of it too, trust me. I try not to, but it is hard. Honestly, it has brought me to some lows. You may ask, "Why do you still use social media then?" Why? Because I love connecting with new people, learning new things, and enjoying fun videos. I even like influencing or being a light to those like you who are kind enough to read this. I've made some great friendships through social media; heck, I even met my husband through it. 

Now why did I say all this? Well, because I wanted to share that yes, we can wish we had what someone else has or the experience they have had, but that doesn't mean we won't. We'll just have our own version. Also, we want to remember to not compare ourselves to others. We want to remember that we don't know their stories or how they got to where they are. We have to enjoy with them, be happy for them. One day, they'll see our special moments and be happy for us too. 




So with that being said, when I get to points in my life where I feel like I'm not getting special experiences, or want what someone else has, I remind myself that I do have great things and have had great opportunities. Sometimes it's the small things that make life so special. 

Here are some things that I think of that help me remember that I am blessed and can help me feel better when I am down. And things to look forward to. 

1. I have my husband

2. I have my two wonderful cats, Travis and Jason

3. We just moved into a new home that is bigger and something we prayed about

4. Jesus, he is always with me and is always there! 


5. I have my job as a teacher assistant. It may not be fancy or the best pay(it's better), but it's a great job that helps kids, and that makes all the difference

6. We have our anniversary trip coming up

7. Great friends and family

8. I can look back on some of the fun and exciting things I have done

9. I can dance and work out. That always makes me feel better

10. I am coming out with a fitness devotional that has a little touch of Disney in it (stay tuned to my social media for info on that)

And I am sure countless more. Whenever I have days that I feel like nothing is going right or that others seems to be "having it all" so to speak, I think about what I have done lately or coming up, and remind myself that it is ok to feel bummed but "pick yourself back up, dust off and keep moving on" there is no sense of having a pitty party and feeling down, it get's you no where. I encourage you to do the same. Remind yourself of the cool things you have done or are going to do, be happy for others' accomplishments or fun adventures, and move on. Enjoy all the little things. 

You got this!! 💪
