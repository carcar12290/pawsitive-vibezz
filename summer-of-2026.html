Summer of 2026

2026-08-10

 Welcome Back to Pawsitive Vibezz! 🐾✨

It's been a little while, hasn't it?

Life has been busy, and somewhere between being on summner break, moving, everyday life, trying to stay active, I started drawing again, and this time on a chalk board, planning our next adventures, and keeping up with two very spoiled cats (who we love so much and are dear to our hearts), my blog took a little bit of a backseat. 




But I've missed it

Pawsitive Vibezz was always meant to be a little corner of the internet where I could share the things I love — Disney, travel, fitness, faith, cats, creativity, and the everyday moments in between.

So I'm bringing it back. ❤️

I have plenty of things I've been wanting to share, and there are some fun adventures coming up that I can't wait to take you along for. 

 

What has been a huge blessing this summer is our move. Man, that was a process, let me tell you. If you moved from a small place to a bigger place, then you understand the work that goes into it. The place we are in now is much bigger. We went from a small 1-bedroom 1-bath to a 2-bedroom 2-bath with a one-car garage, and it's only a two-story building. We are on the first floor. No more climbing stairs or broken elevators! This home is one we were praying about for a long time.         We tried looking elsewhere, but everything was WAY too expensive. One day the Lord opened this one up. We finally got to say yes to a bigger home. When the Lord shows up, he shows up. I am so thankful for his timing and helping me be patient. 


We're also looking ahead to our anniversary trip, which means there will definitely be some Disney planning happening around here. 🏰✨ But I'm saving the full trip recap for after we actually go because I want to be able to share the real experience with you!

And of course, my two gray boys will probably continue making surprise appearances because, let's be honest, they seem to think this is their blog. 😂🐱

So here's to starting again.

More stories. More adventures. More Disney. More creativity. More faith. More positivity.

And hopefully a whole lot more Pawsitive Vibezz.

Thanks for being here. 💕
