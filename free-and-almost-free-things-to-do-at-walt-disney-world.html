Free (and Almost Free!) things to do at Walt Disney World!

2026-08-22


Free (and Almost Free!) Things to Do...

Imagine being able to go to Disney for free! While park tickets aren’t quite free, there are plenty of things to do around Walt Disney World Resort that cost little to nothing.✨

If you’re planning a budget-friendly trip—or just looking for ways to fill your non-park days—this guide is for you.

Free Things to Do at Disney Springs

  • Basin – Stop by the “Try Me” stations to wash your hands with different soaps and scrubs. They smell amazing, and your hands will feel so soft afterward.
  • Ghirardelli Soda Fountain & Chocolate Shop – They’re known for delicious chocolates and often hand out free samples. A sweet little treat!
  • AdventHealth Waterside Stage – Live entertainment is offered most evenings (weather permitting). It’s a great way to relax and enjoy the atmosphere.
  • The LEGO Store – Outside the store, kids (and kids at heart!) can build LEGO creations and race cars on small tracks. You can also create a custom LEGO minifigure of yourself for a fee.
  • Splash Pad – Cool off in the water play area between Once Upon a Toy and Earl of Sandwich.
  • Dino Dig – Let little ones dig for fossils in the sandbox outside T-REX Cafe.
  • Hidden Mickey Hunt – Search for hidden Mickey shapes throughout the area.
  • Get “Spit” On by Stitch – Outside World of Disney, facing the Waterside Stage, you’ll find a larger-than-life Stitch above the entrance. Every so often, he sprays water—kids love trying to get splashed!
  • Coca-Cola Store – Meet the Coca-Cola Polar Bear on the second level for photos. For a small cost, you can sample Coca-Cola products from around the world. I dare you to try Beverly… if you know, you know 😉
  • Disney Springs Art Walk – Explore murals created by local and international artists.
  • Christmas Tree Stroll (Seasonal) – During the holidays, themed Christmas trees are displayed throughout Disney Springs. In past years, guests could collect matching stickers and receive a small prize for completing the stroll. Check the Disney Springs website for current details.
  • Pin Trading – Trade pins with Cast Members or at designated boards (also available at parks and resorts). You’ll need starter pins, which are sold in mystery packs. You can sometimes find authentic Disney pins at resale shops or online—just make sure they include the official Disney logo.
  • M&M's Store – Take photos with colorful characters and keep an eye out for potential samples.

....at Walt Disney World Resort
  • Take a Boat Ride – Hop on a complimentary boat to explore nearby resorts:
    • Disney's Port Orleans Resort – Riverside
    • Disney's Port Orleans Resort – French Quarter
    • Disney's Old Key West Resort
    • Disney's Saratoga Springs Resort & Spa

Resort hopping is such a fun (and relaxing!) way to explore Disney property.

Speaking of Resorts…

Disney resorts offer plenty of amenities. While some are reserved for guests,

everyone can enjoy:

  • Themed lobbies
  • Restaurants and lounges
  • Outdoor lawn games
  • Walking paths
  • Coffee shops
  • Gift shops

Grab a snack or drink and take your time exploring. You’ve heard

of park hopping—try resort hopping on your non-park days!

Low-Cost Fun on Disney Property

If you’re willing to spend just a little, check out the mini golf

courses:


⛳ Winter Summerland Miniature Golf

Located near the Animal Kingdom Resort area, join Santa and his elves

for two festive 18-hole adventures.

Pricing: Around $19 per adult and $12 per child (ages 3–9), plus tax.

⛳ Fantasia Gardens and Fairways Miniature Golf

Located near the Swan Hotel in the EPCOT Resort area, this course is inspired by

Disney’s classic Fantasia.

Pricing: Around $19 per adult and $12 per child (ages 3–9), plus tax.

Free (or Low-Cost) Souvenirs at the Parks

You will need a park ticket for the following activities, but these extras are free once you’re inside.

  • Celebration Buttons & Park Maps
  • At all parks and resorts, you can grab complimentary celebration buttons such as:
  • First Visit
  • Birthday
  • Celebrating
  • Happily Ever After 

A Cast Member will personalize it for you! For anniversaries, we always grab a “Happily Ever After” button and have them write something like “5 Ears” for five years. It makes such a sweet keepsake.

  • For customized designs (starting around $5), visit Ye Olde Christmas Shoppe in Magic Kingdom.

Kidcot Fun Stops

  • At EPCOT, head to World Showcase for Kidcot Fun Stops.

Kids can decorate a small craft at each country pavilion and carry it around

the park for the day.

Festival Passports

  • During EPCOT’s seasonal festivals, you can pick up a complimentary

passport booklet. Each booth stamps it, making it a fun activity for kids

—or a great way to track your favorite bites and drinks.

  • You can also purchase a keepsake festival kit (around $16.99).

Club Cool

  • Located in World Celebration, this Coca-Cola experience offers free

samples of beverages from around the world. It’s a fun (and refreshing)

stop. And yes… try Beverly 😉

Free Ice Water

Skip the water fountains! Any quick-service restaurant at Walt Disney World

will provide a complimentary cup of ice water upon request. Florida

heat is no joke—hydrate often! Bringing your own filtered water bottle is still

a smart idea.

Kids 3 and Under

Children ages 3 and under enter the parks free with a ticketed adult. A big win for families with little ones!

Get “Pixie Dusted”

You can sometimes receive complimentary pixie dust at:

  • Harmony Barber Shop
  • Sir Mickey's
  • Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique

Make a wish while Cast Members sprinkle pixie dust over your head. It’s magical for kids—and kids at heart.

Final Thoughts

Walt Disney World Resort truly has so much to see and do beyond the theme parks. Many of these free experiences are outside the parks, making it easier to plan relaxing, budget-friendly days into your trip.

Yes, you’ll need park tickets for certain activities—but there are still plenty of magical extras included once you’re inside, especially at EPCOT’s World Showcase.
