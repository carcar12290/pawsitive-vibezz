DVC Rentals: Stay in the Magic for WAY Less ✨🏰

Ever dreamed of staying at a Disney deluxe resort… but nearly fainted when you saw the nightly price? SAME.

Good news: you can stay in a deluxe Disney villa for a fraction of the cost — and it’s completely legit. Let’s break down the magic of DVC rentals and how you can level up your stay without blowing your budget.


What Is DVC?

Disney Vacation Club (DVC) is Disney’s version of a timeshare. Members purchase points tied to a “home resort” and use those points for stays at Disney properties.

And here’s the cool part — DVC isn’t just at Walt Disney World! You can also stay at:

  • Hilton Head Island

  • Vero Beach

  • Aulani, A Disney Resort & Spa

But if you’re not ready to commit to a timeshare… you can rent points from a DVC member and enjoy those deluxe perks for much less. 🙌

Why Rent DVC? (Let’s Talk Perks 👀)

When you rent DVC, you’re treated like a deluxe resort guest — which means:

🌙 Extended Evening Hours

Select nights at parks like Magic Kingdom and EPCOT (and occasionally Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom).
 That’s two extra hours in the parks with shorter lines. Yes please.

🌅 Early Theme Park Entry

All Disney resort guests get 30 minutes of early entry every park day. Those early morning rides? Worth it.

💦 Complimentary Water Park Admission

Through summer 2026, Disney resort guests receive a complimentary water park admission on check-in day. (Hello, bonus park day!)

🏡 Bigger Rooms + Kitchenettes

DVC villas often include:

  • Kitchen or kitchenette

  • Mini fridge

  • Microwave

  • Toaster

  • Coffee maker (with coffee + tea)

  • Real cups & utensils

Perfect for families, longer stays, or saving money on meals.

🚌 Free Disney Transportation

Buses, Skyliner, boats, monorail — it’s all included.

How Do You Rent DVC?

You could buy a membership… but if you’re like us and prefer flexibility, renting is the way to go.

We’ve recently used David’s Vacation Club Rentals and found their site easy to navigate and budget-friendly. The process typically looks like this:

  1. Choose your dates and resort preferences

  2. Submit an online reservation request with a small deposit

  3. Receive confirmation

  4. Pay your remaining balance

  5. Link your stay in My Disney Experience

  6. Start your countdown to magic 🎉

Simple, secure, and such a smart way to save.
