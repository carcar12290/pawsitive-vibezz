A Dream Is a Wish

2026-09-12




I have always been asked, "What would your dream job be?" My answer was always "CEO of Disney, or well doing something creative with Disney." and after my response, I'd be asked, "Well, why Disney?" Well, because Disney means a lot to me. It holds a special place in my heart and ver fond memories. It truly is a magical place, even when it is busy and hot. I grew up with Disney and to this day, I still enjoy everything about it. Maybe I'm a Disney adult, I don't truly know, all I know is that I am someone who loves and admires what Disney does. 

To do something creative at Diseny would not only be a dream come true, but would be a huge honor. As farr back as 3 years old, I can remember coloring inside the lines of coloring books. I was always in some kind of art class in school and doing different craft projects. I majored in grpahic design as well. Even in my roll as a teacher assistant, I would find myself doing art projects in art class with the students, even helping them in other classes with projects, or if there is a worksheet that invovles drawing, I am righr there with them. As of recent I started doing chalk drawings, some of jsut things I like and others of Disney snacks, treats, and characters. I wrote a fitness devotional book that even has a little sprinkle of Diseny. So no matter what I do, Disney seems to find it's way into what I do. Everytihng I do, I do it from inspriation and to insprire people. That is why I wrote my book, but also in my drawings and fitness. Disney and people who have or do work for Disney insprire me and I want to do the same.  



No matter what I do or where I go, wether it is with Disney ro not, I want people to see and know that dreams do come true. That you can accomplish big goals and go places. It's just a matter of how you go about it. "Just keep swimming"- Dory  
Don't give up, because if you do, you may never know what could be. Walt didn't give up, he imagined it, and it became a reality. Bigger beyond what he did imagine.
