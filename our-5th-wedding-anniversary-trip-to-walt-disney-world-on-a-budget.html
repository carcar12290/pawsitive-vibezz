 How We Did Walt Disney World on a Budget

A touch of Disney magic

You might be asking, “Carly, how do you do Walt Disney World on a budget?” I’m here to break it down—based on our real-life experience.

Before we start, a quick disclaimer: I’m not a Disney professional or cast member. I just really love Disney, do a lot of research, watch way too much Disney YouTube, and plan trips with intention. What follows is exactly how we made our Disney trip magical without breaking the bank.

Step One: Plan With Purpose

Every Disney trip starts with three big questions:

  • What’s our overall budget?
  • What are our top priorities?
  • When’s the best time to go for us?

Once we know those answers, everything else gets easier—travel, length of stay, and where to splurge vs. save.

Getting There: Fly or Drive?                                                                    EPCOT food and wine festival

We usually fly, because sometimes it’s actually cheaper than driving—but this totally depends on your situation.

A few things to consider:

  • Staying on Disney property all week? You probably won’t need a car.
  • Planning to explore off-property? A rental car might be worth it.
  • Uber adds up fast—we learned it would’ve cost us just as much as renting a car.

For flights, prices can be unpredictable. We were lucky to use airline miles from family (never hurts to ask!). Otherwise, we often use Expedia packages, which bundle flights, hotels, and cars—and sometimes even show Disney resorts for less than Disney’s own website. Always price compare!

Where to Stay Without Overspending

Lodging can be one of the biggest expenses, so research is key. YouTube walkthroughs helped us compare options and prices.

We were lucky enough to stay with my aunt in Florida for most of our trip (huge savings!). If that wasn’t an option, we would’ve chosen:

  • Family
  • Airbnb
  • Budget-friendly Disney resorts
  • Or DVC rentals (more on that below)
(Yes, It’s Possible!)

Our last night celebrating at the Edison


Staying on Disney Property for Less: DVC Rentals

Let’s talk about one of our biggest budget wins: Disney Vacation Club (DVC) rentals.

Before you think, “I can’t afford a timeshare,” don’t worry—you’re not buying one. You’re renting points, and it can save you hundreds.

Our DVC Stays:

  • Old Key West (Moderate Resort)
    • Paid: $180 total
    • Disney price: ~$424/night
    • Saved: $244
  • Copper Creek at Wilderness Lodge (Deluxe Resort)
    • Paid: $299/night
    • Disney price: ~$753/night
    • Saved: $454

✨ Two nights, two resorts, $479 total—basically a BOGO stay.

DVC Perks We Loved:

  • Free resort parking
  • Free Disney transportation
  • Early park entry
  • Free water park day on check-in
  • Optional DVC tour = $200 Disney gift card- depending on what they offer at the time

If staying on property feels out of reach, DVC rentals are a fantastic way to experience Disney magic for less.

Another Budget-Friendly Option: Fort Wilderness Camping

If you love the outdoors, check out Disney’s Fort Wilderness campsites. I’ve seen them as low as $55 per night! Options range from tent sites to full RV hookups—and you still get Disney resort perks.

Park Days, Priorities & Smart Spending

Disney has so much to do—4 parks, 2 water parks, 25+ resorts, mini golf, and Disney Springs. You’ll never do it all, so focus on what matters most.

Our Top Priorities:

  • Animal Kingdom (Avatar, Dinosaur before it closes in 2026)
  • EPCOT during Food & Wine Festival
  • Mickey’s Not-So-Scary Halloween Party (our main reason for the trip!)

🎃 Pro tip: Special ticketed events mean shorter wait times, exclusive shows, and tons of candy—worth planning around.

Festival Tips That Save Money 


If you’re visiting EPCOT during a festival:

  • Check the concert schedule—big names = bigger crowds.
  • Grab a festival wristband gift card. It’s a souvenir and keeps spending in check.

We also:

  • Share festival bites
  • Bring snacks and refillable water bottles
  • Order kids’ meals (still plenty of food, way cheaper)

Save Even More With Resort Days 

One of our favorite budget (and sanity-saving) tricks? Resort days. 

We love doing every other day in the parks:

  • Sleep in
  • Swim
  • Relax sore feet
  • Cook meals at home

Then we’ll head to Disney Springs for shopping, live entertainment, and vibes ✨. Grocery delivery and DoorDash make this super easy.

Magical Moments That Cost Us Nothing                                                     Free day at Blizzard Beach

Some of our favorite memories were completely free:

  • Free car upgrade from Enterprise (anniversary shirts!)
  • Special anniversary message on our resort TV
  • Free dessert at Whispering Canyon Café
  • $200 Disney gift card from a DVC tour
  • Free water park day
  • Surprise Lightning Lane for Cosmic Rewind
  • Anniversary shout-outs at The Edison
  • Walk-up bar seating at Space 220

🎉 All thanks to celebration pins!

                                                                                                    

These pins are FREE—just ask a Cast Member at guest services at your resort. You can even customize them at Ye Olde Christmas Shoppe in Magic Kingdom for a small fee.

Final Thoughts: Disney Magic Is Possible on a Budget

However you plan your Disney trip, just know this: there are always options. With a little research, flexibility, and creativity, you can have an unforgettable, magical Disney vacation—without overspending.
